## About Trancehotels

**Trancehotels** is a **Skating Rink** in **Boston** — Rink with rentals and skilled skate guards.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

---

## Why we exist

Trancehotels exists to make skating rink feel personal in Boston — clear pricing, honest recommendations, and a team that remembers why you came back.

- Rooted in Boston with regulars who know our name
- Offerings that match what we publish online
- Support you can reach during real hours
- A space built for repeat visits, not one-offs

---

## Where we're headed

We're growing Trancehotels into a entertainment people recommend without hesitation — more depth in our craft, same straightforward experience at the door.
